Put the official qBotica logo here, exported from the brand kit:

  qbotica-logo.svg   (preferred)
  or qbotica-logo.png (transparent background, at least 120 px tall)

Use the standard horizontal logo for light backgrounds. The app picks it up
automatically on refresh. Until then, the header shows the name as plain text.

Per the brand guidelines: don't redraw, recolor, tilt, or rearrange the logo,
and keep clear space around it at least the height of the "a" in the logotype.
